## Full description (max 4000)

```
Champions Teamsheet Generator turns the two in-game Pokémon Champions screenshots — the Stats
screen and the Moves & More screen — into everything you need to register a team, in seconds.

WHAT IT DOES
• Reads your two screenshots with on-device text recognition (OCR).
• Builds a ready-to-paste Pokémon Showdown / PokePaste team.
• Generates the official Open Team Sheet (OTS) and the Staff Team Sheet as PDFs, filled from your
  player details and your team.
• Lets you review and fix anything the reader was unsure about before you export.

WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
No account. No servers. Everything runs on your phone — your screenshots and player details never
leave the device. Once installed, it works with no internet connection at all.

BUILT FOR TOURNAMENTS
• Saves player profiles on your device (name, Player ID, date of birth, division, in-game and
  Switch profile names) so you don't retype them every event.
• Handles the Champions specifics: level 50, EV totals out of 66, natures, held items and gender.
• Print both sheets straight from the app, or download the two PDFs to submit.

HOW TO USE
1. Add your player profile and enter the Battle Team number/name.
2. Pick the two in-game screenshots (any order — the app tells them apart).
3. Review the read-out and correct anything highlighted.
4. Export the Showdown paste and the OTS / Staff PDFs.

Play Pokémon VGC and speak Portuguese? Join the Portuguese VGC community on Discord:
https://discord.gg/u428smyhu

## Data safety form (answers)

- Does your app collect or share user data? **No.**
- All processing is on-device; nothing is transmitted. Declare no data collected and no data
  shared. (Profiles are stored only in the app's local storage on the device.)

## Privacy policy

Google requires a URL even when you collect nothing. A one-paragraph page suffices, e.g. host it
on the existing homelab domain. Suggested text:

> Champions Teamsheet Generator does not collect, store, or share any personal data off your
> device. Screenshots you load and the player profiles you enter are processed entirely on your
> device and are saved only in the app's local storage. The app requires no account and sends no
> data to any server. Contact: <your email>.
