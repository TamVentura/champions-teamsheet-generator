Privacy Policy — Champions Teamsheet Generator

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Champions Teamsheet Generator ("the app") is an offline tool that turns two in-game Pokémon Champions screenshots into a Pokémon Showdown paste and the official team sheet PDFs. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not gather your name, email, location, device identifiers, or usage data.

How your content is processed

The screenshots you load and the player details you enter (such as player name, Player ID, date of birth and division) are processed entirely on your device. Text recognition (OCR) and PDF generation run locally, offline. This information is never transmitted off your device.

Where your data is stored

Player profiles you create are saved only in the app's local storage on your device, so you don't have to retype them. You can delete them at any time from within the app. Generated PDFs are saved only where you choose to save or share them.

Permissions

The app reads the image files you explicitly pick, in order to process them. It requires no other sensitive permissions and needs no internet connection to function.

Third parties

The app does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect or transmit any data.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
